EchoResta, hospitality school in Abidjan

Home/About us

Our story

EchoResta was founded by Ivorian hotel and restaurant professionals, convinced that a service profession is learned first and foremost by practising it. Since our first graduating classes, we have combined theory classes, practical workshops and on-the-job immersion.

Today, more than 2,500 trainees have been trained and work in the hotels, restaurants and partner establishments of Abidjan and the wider region.

  • Trainers from the industry, still working in the field
  • Dual international certification (Côte d’Ivoire & Australia)
  • Guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels
See our courses
What we teach

Ten hotel and restaurant professions

EchoResta trains people for the jobs most in demand by Abidjan’s hotels, restaurants, lounges and caterers: cook, waiter and hostess, maître d’hôtel, bartender, receptionist, room attendant and valet and housekeeper.

For professionals who want to move up, the school also offers courses in hotel management, restaurant management and English for tourism and hospitality.

The school is located at Angré Star 14, in the district of Cocody. Its courses are open to candidates from every district of Abidjan — Cocody, Yopougon, Abobo, Adjamé, Plateau, Marcory, Koumassi, Treichville, Port-Bouët, Bingerville… — as well as from the rest of the country and from West African countries.

Our method

A teaching approach centred on practice

Classes to understand

The foundations of the job: hygiene and safety standards, establishment standards, professional vocabulary, management and organisation.

Workshops to practise

In the kitchen, dining room, bar and guest rooms, you repeat the skills of the job in small groups, with a trainer who corrects you on the spot.

Immersion to enter the workplace

The guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels puts you in real working conditions and prepares you for employment.

Mission, vision and values

Our mission

To train professionals who are ready to work from day one and able to meet the standards of international hospitality.

Our vision

To make Abidjan a regional benchmark for hospitality excellence, by raising the standard of the whole profession.

Our values

Rigour, a sense of service, kindness and passing on knowledge: what our trainers practise every day at work.

After the course

Support towards employment

The course does not end with the award of the dual international certification (Côte d’Ivoire and Australia). The guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels is the first step towards a job: you discover how an establishment really runs and you make your work known.

EchoResta also runs a recruitment area: hotels, restaurants and private individuals describe their staffing needs, hospitality and catering professionals submit their form, and our team puts them in touch.

The school welcomes pupils and students, unemployed people, employees of the hospitality industry or another sector, and entrepreneurs. The enrolment form accepts candidates from all over West Africa.

Frequently asked questions about the school

What is EchoResta?

EchoResta is a hospitality and catering training school located at Angré Star 14, in the district of Cocody, Abidjan. It offers ten professional courses, from room attendant to hotel manager, including cooking, dining room service, bar and English.

Who can enrol at the school?

Pupils and students, unemployed people, employees of the hospitality industry or another sector, and entrepreneurs. The level required is stated on each course; candidates under 18 enrol with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Are the trainers industry professionals?

Yes. Classes and workshops are led by industry professionals, chefs, maîtres d’hôtel, housekeepers, who work in Abidjan’s establishments.

Can I visit the school before enrolling?

Yes. Book an appointment online, Monday to Friday, for a visit, a guidance interview or enrolment.
