Waiter and hostess training in Abidjan

Home/Courses/Waiter / Hostess

The smile that makes guests want to come back: a people-facing job you can start quickly, in demand everywhere.

Restaurants

Welcome, advise and serve with elegance, from setting up the dining room to taking payment. A hands-on course, led by working professionals, so you are ready from your very first job.

Why take this course in Abidjan?

Restaurants, hotels, caterers and events: waiting tables is one of the most in-demand jobs in Abidjan’s restaurant industry. Our waiter and hostess course teaches you how to welcome guests, serve at the table and sell, so you are ready from your very first job and can move up to chef de rang or maître d’hôtel.

What do a waiter and a hostess do day to day?

The waiter or waitress welcomes guests, seats them, presents the menu, takes the order, passes it to the kitchen, serves food and drinks, then takes payment. The hostess greets guests when they arrive, manages bookings and shows them to their tables. The job is done as part of a team, often in the evening and at weekends, with busy peaks that call for stamina, a good memory and a smile.

What you will be able to do

  • Welcome and seat guests
  • Present the menu and give advice
  • Take orders and pass them on to the kitchen
  • Serve plated dishes and carry trays
  • Take payment and reset the table

How does the course work?

Workshops take place in the dining room: setting up and laying tables, welcoming guests and customer relations, taking orders and calling them to the kitchen, plated, platter and tray service, serving drinks and the basics of wine, taking payment with a till system. Every session has you practise the skills of service under the eye of a trainer from the industry.

Frequently asked questions about the waiter / hostess course

Who is the “Waiter / Hostess” course in Abidjan for?

Level required: Beginner. The course is open to candidates from Abidjan, from all over Côte d’Ivoire and from West African countries. Candidates under 18 enrol with the consent of a parent or guardian.

What jobs can I do after the “Waiter / Hostess” course?

After the course, you can start as a waiter or waitress, then move up to chef de rang, maître d’hôtel and dining room manager. You can work in: restaurants, hotels, caterers and events or VIP lounges.

Does the “Waiter / Hostess” course lead to a certification?

Yes. It leads to a dual international certification (Côte d’Ivoire and Australia) and ends with a guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels.

Where does the course take place?

At the EchoResta school, Angré Star 14, Cocody (Abidjan), Monday to Friday between 8 am and 5 pm. Classes combine theory, practical workshops and on-the-job immersion, supervised by industry professionals.

How do I enrol in the “Waiter / Hostess” course?

Fill in the online enrolment form, then prepare your file: 2 recent passport photos, 1 copy of your ID document and 1 CV. You can also book an appointment or call us on +225 07 68 84 20 38.

What is the difference between a waiter and a hostess?

The waiter or waitress handles table service: orders, dishes, drinks, payment. The hostess greets guests on arrival, manages bookings and shows them to their tables. The two jobs complement each other in a restaurant or hotel and are practised together in the course.

How can I progress after working as a waiter?

With experience, a waiter becomes a chef de rang, then a maître d’hôtel and dining room manager. The Maître d’hôtel course is designed for this step: guéridon service, food and wine pairing, running the dining room.

Do I need to speak English to work in a dining room?

It is not compulsory to start, but it is a real asset in hotels and restaurants with international guests. The English for tourism & hospitality course exists for this; for a programme combining several courses, contact us.
