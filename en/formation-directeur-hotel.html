Hotel manager training in Abidjan

Home/Courses/Hotel manager

Running a hotel is like running a small town: guests, teams, budget and reputation.

Hospitality

Strategy, finance, revenue management, human resources and quality: everything you need to run a hotel. A hands-on course, led by working professionals, so you are ready from your very first job.

Why take this course in Abidjan?

Business tourism and the major international events hosted in Abidjan create a need for managers able to run an establishment. This hotel management course gives you the tools of hotel leadership: sales strategy, revenue management, financial management, human resources and service quality.

What you will be able to do

  • Define the hotel’s sales strategy
  • Manage the budget and profitability
  • Recruit, lead and retain teams
  • Guarantee service quality and online reputation
  • Represent the hotel with partners

The programme

  1. Hotel strategy and marketingPositioning, target customers, action plan.
  2. Revenue managementPricing, sales channels and online bookings.
  3. Financial managementBudget, operating account and dashboards.
  4. Human resourcesRecruitment, schedules, employment law, motivation.
  5. Quality and online reputationGuest reviews, standards and service culture.
  6. RegulationsHygiene, safety and the establishment’s obligations.

A typical day

  1. 8:00Reviewing the previous day’s indicators
  2. 9:00Heads of department meeting
  3. 11:00Meeting with a corporate client
  4. 15:00Budget and revenue management review
  5. 18:00Walk around the hotel and talking with guests

Frequently asked questions about the hotel manager course

Who is the “Hotel manager” course in Abidjan for?

Level required: Bac +2 or hotel experience. The course is open to candidates from Abidjan, from all over Côte d’Ivoire and from West African countries. Candidates under 18 enrol with the consent of a parent or guardian.

What jobs can I do after the “Hotel manager” course?

After the course, you can start as an executive assistant, then move up to deputy manager, hotel manager and regional director. You can work in: independent hotels, hotel chains, lodges and residences or tourism groups.

Does the “Hotel manager” course lead to a certification?

Yes. It leads to a dual international certification (Côte d’Ivoire and Australia) and ends with a guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels.

Where does the course take place?

At the EchoResta school, Angré Star 14, Cocody (Abidjan), Monday to Friday between 8 am and 5 pm. Classes combine theory, practical workshops and on-the-job immersion, supervised by industry professionals.

How do I enrol in the “Hotel manager” course?

Fill in the online enrolment form, then prepare your file: 2 recent passport photos, 1 copy of your ID document and 1 CV. You can also book an appointment or call us on +225 07 68 84 20 38.
