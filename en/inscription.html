Enrolment


Frequently asked questions about enrolment








Which documents are needed for the enrolment file?
2 recent passport photos, 1 copy of your ID document and 1 CV. Our team will tell you how to send them once your form has been reviewed.


Can I enrol before the age of 18?
Yes. Candidates under 18 give the contact details of a parent or guardian in the enrolment form.


Can I apply from another West African country?
Yes. The form accepts candidates from all West African countries; courses take place in Abidjan, Angré Star 14, Cocody.


What happens after I send the form?
Our team reviews your request and then calls you to arrange an enrolment interview at the school. For any questions: +225 07 68 84 20 38.
