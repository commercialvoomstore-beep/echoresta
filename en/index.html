A few words about EchoResta

A hospitality and catering training school based in Abidjan, EchoResta has for several years been preparing professionals who are ready to work from day one. Cooking, dining room service, bar, reception, accommodation and professional English: every programme is led by practitioners from the industry.

Dual international certification (Côte d’Ivoire & Australia) and a guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels.

EchoResta was born from a conviction: by sharing what we know how to do, we raise the whole profession.

Your school in Abidjan

Train for hotel and restaurant careers in Abidjan

Every year Abidjan welcomes many business travellers, tourists and major events, and new hotels and restaurants open regularly. These establishments are looking for staff trained to international standards.

A hospitality and catering school located at Angré Star 14, in the district of Cocody, EchoResta prepares students for the most sought-after jobs: cook, waiter and hostess, receptionist, room attendant and valet, housekeeper, bartender, maître d’hôtel, as well as hotel management, restaurant management and professional English. Our courses are open to trainees from every district of Abidjan and from across the country.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the EchoResta hospitality school in Abidjan?

EchoResta is located at Angré Star 14, in the district of Cocody, Abidjan (Google location code: 92JH+FW). The school is open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm.

Which hospitality and catering courses do you offer?

Ten courses: valet and room attendant, housekeeper, receptionist and night auditor, hotel manager, waiter and hostess, maître d’hôtel, cook, bartending and mixology, restaurant manager, and English for hospitality and tourism.

Are the courses recognised?

Every programme leads to a dual international certification (Côte d’Ivoire and Australia) and ends with a guaranteed 6-month internship in the finest hotels.

How do I enrol in a course?

Fill in the online enrolment form, call us or message us on WhatsApp at +225 07 68 84 20 38. An advisor will call you back for an enrolment interview.
